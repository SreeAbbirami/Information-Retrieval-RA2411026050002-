Artificial intelligence is the simulation of human intelligence processes by machines, especially computer systems. Artificial intelligence applications include expert systems, natural language processing, speech recognition and machine vision. Artificial intelligence requires a foundation of specialized hardware and software for writing and training machine learning algorithms. Artificial intelligence focuses on three cognitive skills: learning, reasoning and self-correction. The learning aspect of artificial intelligence processes focuses on acquiring data and creating rules for how to turn it into actionable information. Artificial intelligence has become a collective term for applications that perform complex tasks that once required human input. The term artificial intelligence was coined in 1956 at a conference. Artificial intelligence has gone through many cycles of optimism, followed by disappointment and loss of funding. Artificial intelligence is important because it can give enterprises insights into their operations that they may not have been aware of previously. Advanced artificial intelligence techniques are being used to improve customer experiences, automate processes, and increase productivity. Artificial intelligence systems are becoming more accurate and efficient because they learn from previous interactions.
