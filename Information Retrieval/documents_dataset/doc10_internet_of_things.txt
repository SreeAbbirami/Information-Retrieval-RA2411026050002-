The Internet of Things describes the network of physical objects or things that are embedded with sensors, software, and other technologies for the purpose of connecting and exchanging data with other devices and systems over the internet. These devices range from ordinary household objects to sophisticated industrial tools. With more than 10 billion connected IoT devices today, the Internet of Things is becoming one of the most important technologies in the 21st century. The Internet of Things makes it possible to connect everyday objects to the internet via embedded devices, creating seamless communication between people, processes, and things. IoT enables communication between devices without requiring human intervention. The benefits of IoT for businesses include the ability to monitor their overall business processes, improve the customer experience, save time and money, enhance employee productivity, adapt and integrate business models, and make better business decisions. IoT applications span across many industries including smart homes, smart cities, manufacturing, agriculture, healthcare, transportation and retail. IoT devices generate massive amounts of data that can be analyzed to provide actionable insights. IoT security is a growing concern as more devices connect to vulnerable networks.
