Computer vision is a field of artificial intelligence that enables computers and systems to derive meaningful information from digital images, videos and other visual inputs. Computer vision trains machines to perform these functions, but it has to do it in much less time with cameras, data and algorithms rather than retinas, optic nerves and a visual cortex. Computer vision works similarly to human vision, except humans have a head start. Computer vision trains machines to perform visual recognition tasks from millions of images in mere seconds. Computer vision uses convolutional neural networks. The convolutional neural network breaks the image down into tiny pixels that it can analyze. Computer vision has many real-world applications. Self-driving vehicles integrate computer vision to recognize lanes, signs, and other vehicles. Computer vision also enables facial recognition to match people's faces. Computer vision is used in manufacturing and healthcare industries. Manufacturing companies use computer vision to spot product defects before shipping. Medical imagery analysis uses computer vision to find abnormalities in scans and x-rays. Computer vision is also a key technology behind augmented reality and virtual reality experiences.
