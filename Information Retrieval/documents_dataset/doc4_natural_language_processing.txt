Natural language processing is a branch of artificial intelligence that helps computers understand, interpret and manipulate human language. Natural language processing helps computers communicate with humans in their own language and scales other language-related tasks. Natural language processing makes it possible for computers to read text, hear speech, interpret it, measure sentiment and determine which parts are important. Current approaches to natural language processing are based on deep learning, which can automatically extract meaning from text and speech. Many different types of human-computer interfaces and natural language processing tasks exist. Natural language processing includes many different methods for interpreting human language, ranging from statistical and machine learning methods to rules-based and algorithmic approaches. Natural language processing tasks include speech recognition, part-of-speech tagging, word sense disambiguation, named entity recognition, sentiment analysis and natural language generation. Natural language processing in business intelligence tools search through massive volumes of unstructured, text-heavy data. Applications for natural language processing include interactive voice response systems for customer service applications. Natural language processing has been used in fraud detection. Natural language processing also plays a key role in enterprise tools, chatbots and assistive technology.
