Robotics is the intersection of science, engineering and technology that produces machines, called robots, that substitute for or replicate human actions. Robotics is a branch of engineering that involves the conception, design, manufacture and operation of robots. This field overlaps with electronics, computer science, artificial intelligence, mechatronics, nanotechnology and bioengineering. Robots are used in many industries for various tasks. Industrial robots are used for manufacturing and assembly lines to perform repetitive tasks with high precision and speed. Collaborative robots work alongside humans in shared workspaces. Service robots assist with healthcare, hospitality, cleaning and personal assistance. Robots have electrical components that power and control the machinery. Robots contain at least some level of computer programming to perform actions. Without a set of codes telling a robot what to do, it would be just another piece of simple machinery. Programming creates intelligence in robots. The field of robotics has grown significantly with advances in artificial intelligence and machine learning. Autonomous robots can operate independently in unstructured environments without continuous human guidance. Robotics research continues to push boundaries with humanoid robots, swarm robotics and soft robotics.
