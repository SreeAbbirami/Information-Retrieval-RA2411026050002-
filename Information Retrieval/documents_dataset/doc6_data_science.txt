Data science combines multiple fields including statistics, scientific methods, artificial intelligence and data analysis to extract value from data. Data science is an interdisciplinary field that uses scientific methods, processes, algorithms and systems to extract knowledge and insights from noisy, structured and unstructured data. Data science is related to data mining, machine learning and big data. Data science is a concept to unify statistics, data analysis and their related methods in order to understand and analyze actual phenomena with data. Data scientists are analytics experts who use their technical skills in solving complex problems and their social skills to find solutions. Data scientists are often tasked with turning big data into business value. Data science typically works with large amounts of data through mining, cleaning and analyzing data. The data science process involves understanding the problem, acquiring the data, processing and exploring the data, modeling, and presenting the results. Tools used by data scientists include Python, R, SQL, SAS and various machine learning libraries. Data science is being used across many industries including healthcare, finance, marketing and retail. Applications of data science include recommendation systems, fraud detection, personalized medicine and predictive maintenance.
